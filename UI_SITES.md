## 💡 Quick Workflows for Building Next-Gen Sites

1. **Ideation & Moodboard**: Browse **Godly**, **Recent Design**, and **Land-book** to establish visual direction, grid structure, and color temperature.
2. **Hero & Landing Architecture**: Pull structure from **SupaHero**, **Landdding**, and **Scrolltide** for 3D camera tracks, hero headlines, and conversion pacing.
3. **Navigation & Conversion Anatomy**: Style floating menus via **Navbar Gallery**, CTAs with **CTA Gallery**, and clean sitemaps with **Footer Design**.
4. **Visual Effects & 3D Assets**: Integrate glassmorphism from **Glass by Sam Asante** and 3D icons from **3dicons.co**.
5. **AI Generation & Prompts**: Pull structured layouts and background prompts from **MotionSites** and **Scrolltide** directly into your AI coding assistant.
6. **Motion & Interaction**: Reference **60fps.design** and **React Bits** for physics-based springs, magnetic hover pulls, and scroll-linked animations.
7. **Brand Typography**: Match luxury or industrial branding using **FontInLogo** and real product systems from **Styles by Refero**.
